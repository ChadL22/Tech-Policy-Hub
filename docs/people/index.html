Personal WebsiteResearch Website

Sipho C. Langa (co-lead, M.S.) is a research engineer at SAS with experience spanning industry, academia, and government, including MIT, Princeton's Center for Information Technology Policy, the Federal Reserve Board, and the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau. His research examines questions at the intersection of technology, law, and policy through the lens of mechanism design, drawing particularly from welfare economics, consumer protection, and human-computer interaction.

He directs the Center for Governance of Technology and Systems (GoTech) and is an Associate Research Professor in UMD's School of Public Policy, researching how to measure the effects of cyberattacks to help policymakers assess strategic risk.

Mark McDermott is a researcher and engineer with over 15 years of experience spanning cybersecurity, disinformation, machine learning, and big data. His work crosses mathematics, engineering, and analysis, from U.S. defense applications to international research collaborations.

Dr. Michelle Mazurek

Affiliate
CYCP

Michelle Mazurek is an associate professor of computer science at the University of Maryland and Director of the Maryland Cybersecurity Center. Her research focuses on human-centered computer security, understanding and improving how professionals and everyday users learn, adopt, and experience security and privacy practices.

Dr. Gabriel Kaptchuk

Affiliate
CY

Gabriel Kaptchuk is an assistant professor in UMD's Department of Computer Science, with a joint appointment in the University of Maryland Institute for Advanced Computer Studies (UMIACS). His research centers on human-centered cryptography, making privacy-preserving systems intuitive and accessible.

Parthav Poudel

He conducts research at UMD's College of Information on data infrastructure, web privacy, and misinformation, building web-crawling and data pipelines that inform cybersecurity and misinformation policy.
